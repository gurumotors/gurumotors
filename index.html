FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your service.

How often should I service my vehicle?+
Typically every 10,000 km or as recommended by your vehicle manufacturer. Factors like driving conditions, vehicle age, and type of use can also affect service intervals. We can advise you on the best schedule for your specific vehicle.
Do you service all vehicle brands?+
Yes! We service all major manufacturers including Toyota, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Hyundai, Maruti Suzuki, Tata, Mahindra, and many more — including luxury and commercial vehicles.
Do you provide vehicle diagnostics?+
Absolutely. We use advanced diagnostic equipment for accurate fault detection. Our ECU scanners identify issues with the engine, transmission, ABS, airbags, and all electronic systems with precision.
Can I book an appointment online?+
Yes! Use the booking form on this page, or call us at +91 9845577770 / +91 7349666431. We also accept WhatsApp and Instagram bookings for convenience.
Do you use genuine spare parts?+
We use genuine OEM parts wherever possible, along with high-quality aftermarket alternatives from reputable brands. We discuss all part options transparently before proceeding.
What are your working hours?+
Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM – 7:30 PM. Sundays: Emergency appointments only. We recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time slot.
